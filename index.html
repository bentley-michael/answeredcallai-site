08 FAQ

The practical questions.

What happens when I answer the call?

AnsweredCall is configured around missed-call forwarding. When you answer normally, the call stays with you.

What information can it collect?

Your setup defines the questions. The current intake supports details such as name, callback number, address, requested service, issue description, and urgency.

How do I receive the lead?

Lead details can be delivered by SMS, email, or Google Sheets, depending on the setup you choose.

Does it replace my business number?

No. Setup uses missed-call forwarding from your main business phone to the AnsweredCall number assigned to your intake.

Can I test it before activation?

Yes. Onboarding includes a test-call window and acceptance checks for the greeting, transfers, emergency handling, and lead delivery.
