Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 2026

Who We Are

AnsweredCall is operated by Michael Bentley as a sole proprietor. We provide AI-assisted phone intake and voicemail capture services for home service contractors. Our website is https://answeredcall.us.

Information We Collect

When you use our services or interact with us, we may collect:

  • Phone numbers submitted through our contact or signup forms
  • Message content from SMS interactions, including voicemail transcripts and call summaries
  • Business contact information you provide, such as name, company name, and email address

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information solely to deliver the services you requested:

  • Processing voicemail transcripts and delivering structured lead data to Google Sheets
  • Sending SMS notifications and service-related updates
  • Responding to your inquiries and providing support

We do not use your information for advertising or unrelated marketing purposes.

SMS Communications

By providing your phone number and requesting our services, you consent to receive SMS messages from AnsweredCall related to your service setup and account. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP at any time to opt out. Reply HELP for support.

No Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or any purpose other than delivering the services described above. We do not share your data with data brokers.

Mobile information and SMS consent data will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text message opt-in data and consent are used only to provide communications related to your inquiry, demo request, service updates, and customer support.

Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary to provide our services. You may request deletion of your information at any time by contacting us at the address below.

Contact & Data Removal

To request removal of your data or for any privacy-related questions, contact us at sales@answeredcall.us or visit https://answeredcall.us.
